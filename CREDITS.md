## Artwork

The official `assets/branding/onlygoblins-logo.png` was supplied by the maintainer, who confirmed ownership or redistribution permission. The mascot icon is a deterministic crop of that artwork. The banner places the complete artwork on a dark background. The original supplied PNG is kept byte-for-byte. Icons crop and resize its face without redrawing the artwork. The 1280×640 preview fits the complete image on a dark background. The maintainer supplies these OnlyGoblins branding assets under the repository's GPL-3.0 terms.

The original upstream icon files remain in the source under `resources/` for provenance. The public executable uses the OnlyGoblins artwork. An inherited third-party reference PDF is retained locally and excluded from publication pending a separate permission check.
